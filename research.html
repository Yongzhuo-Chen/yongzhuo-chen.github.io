RESEARCH

Methods and biological questions, together.

I work across statistics and computational biology, developing methods for borrowing information and applying them to longitudinal single-cell and genomic data.

Statistical methodology

01 / TRANSFER LEARNING

Late-fusion multi-task learning

Developed an adaptive late-fusion approach for heterogeneous studies without individual-level data exchange, extending transfer to nuisance functions and investigating estimation guarantees. Applications include mammography-screening trials.

JASA · Accepted · Theory and Methods · More details →
02 / DYNAMIC SYSTEMS

Local transfer for time-varying networks

Developed adaptive estimators and inference procedures for transition and precision matrices, with applications to longitudinal human brain organoid data and evolving gene-expression relationships.

Statistical inference · Brain organoids

Computational genomics

03 / ALS GENOMICS

MCformer: regulatory variant effects

Adapted AlphaGenome to model cell-type-specific expression in the human motor cortex. Used in silico variant perturbations and genetic association analyses to investigate potential regulatory mechanisms of ALS.

Ongoing research · Genomic foundation models
04 / SINGLE-CELL AI

scGMP: disease genes and cell types

Combined cell and gene embeddings from pretrained single-cell foundation models in an attention-based neural network to predict GWAS-associated disease genes and prioritize disease-relevant cell types. Explored applications to Alzheimer’s disease.

Exploratory research · Alzheimer’s disease
05 / MULTIOMIC COLLABORATION

ALS primary motor cortex atlas

Applied single-cell disease relevance score (scDRS) analysis to ALS survival-associated genetic signals, identifying enrichment in oligodendrocytes, astrocytes, and microglia.

Collaborative research · RNA/ATAC atlas · More details →
