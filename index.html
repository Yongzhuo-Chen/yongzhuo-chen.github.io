HELLO, I’M YONGZHUO.

From biological complexity to precision medicine.

My research aims to develop statistical and AI-driven methods to understand gene regulation during human development and disease. I study how to selectively integrate information across heterogeneous studies, populations, and time points for reliable inference, and use genomic foundation models to connect genetic variation, cellular dynamics, and disease mechanisms.

About me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Biostatistics at the University of Florida, advised by Dr. Muxuan Liang and Dr. Sai Zhang. My work lies at the intersection of statistical inference, adaptive transfer learning, single-cell genomics, and genomic foundation models. I am especially interested in methods that support reliable scientific discovery across heterogeneous biomedical datasets.

My research spans methodological questions—such as estimation and inference for time-varying systems—and computational biology applications involving the human brain, noncoding variants, and neurodegenerative disease.
